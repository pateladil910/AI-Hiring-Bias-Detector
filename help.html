Help & Support Center

Frequently asked questions about EquiHire AI assessments.

How is my composite score calculated?

Your composite score is computed using: Composite = (MCQ × 0.4) + (Coding × 0.4) + (ResumeMatch × 0.2). This ensures balanced weighting across aptitude, algorithmic skill, and domain alignment.

What if my assessment timer disconnects?

Your answers autosave every 30 seconds to the database. If you refresh or experience network interruptions, you can resume your active test from your candidate dashboard.

How do recruiters view my profile?

Recruiters view an anonymized card displaying your reference ID (e.g., CAND-8A4F), test scores, and extracted skills. Your personal contact information is never shown during screening.
