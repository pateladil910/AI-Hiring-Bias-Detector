    GOT QUESTIONS?

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Does EquiHire AI completely eliminate bias? ▼

    Bias reduction is a process support tool, not a guarantee. We provide systems that limit demographic leakage and create objective metrics, but hiring decisions still rely on contextual human judgment.

    How does demographic redaction work? ▼

    Our AI parsing engine scans uploaded resumes and automatically redacts Name, Age, Gender pronouns, Photo, Address, and University prestige before evaluators or recruiters see the profile.

    How is the composite merit score calculated? ▼

    Scores are calculated across MCQ accuracy, hands-on coding AST test case results, and resume skill vector alignment.
