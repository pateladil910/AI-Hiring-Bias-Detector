Accessible Design Principles

EquiHire AI is built to ensure that technical evaluations are free of accessibility barriers. We actively conform with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA criteria.

  • Full Keyboard Navigation: Complete test workflows, domain pickers, and code submissions are accessible via Tab, Enter, and Arrow navigation.
  • Visible Focus Rings: Prominent 3px emerald outline indicators ensure keyboard users always understand active focus position.
  • Reduced Motion: Full obedience to the prefers-reduced-motion operating system setting, disabling non-essential transitions.
  • Contrast Ratios: Text elements maintain at least 4.5:1 contrast against surface containers.
For accommodations or assistance, contact accessibility@fairhire.io.
