Privacy Policy & Demographic Cloaking Charter

Version 3.0.0 • Updated September 2026

1. Automated PII Redaction

All resumes uploaded to EquiHire AI are processed through our multi-pattern regex engine before any human evaluator can access them. Personal names, email addresses, telephone numbers, postal addresses, gendered pronouns, age indicators, and photo references are permanently stripped.

2. Candidate GDPR Rights

Under GDPR Article 17 and Article 20, candidates retain full authority to download their assessment benchmarks or request complete data erasure directly from their dashboard profile.

3. Immutable Audit Trails

All recruiter interactions, score generations, and authorized identity reveals are logged in our tamper-evident audit database to guarantee EEOC regulatory transparency.
