OUR MISSION & PHILOSOPHY

Building Objective & Verifiable Hiring Pipelines

EquiHire AI was founded on the core principle that hiring decisions should be based purely on technical skill, experience, and logical problem-solving ability — without demographic bias.

The Problem with Traditional Screening

Unconscious human bias leads to systemic rejection of qualified candidates based on name origin, gender indicators, age, geographic location, or university brand names.

The EquiHire AI Solution

We implement automated demographic isolation engines, objective timed skill sandboxes, and explainable audit trails compliant with EEOC Guidelines and GDPR data protection laws.

Evaluation Methodology Timeline

1

Demographic Anonymization Engine

Parsing algorithms strip candidate names, photos, gender pronouns, addresses, and university names prior to skill graph extraction.

2

Domain Skill Graph Vectoring

Technical proficiencies and years of experience are mapped against domain requirements to calculate objective skill match percentages.

3

Timed Candidate Assessment

Timed MCQ, logical reasoning, and hands-on coding challenges are scored deterministically by automated AST parsers.

4

EEOC & Bias Audit Reporting

Every candidate evaluation generates a tamper-proof audit log verifying 0.00% demographic leakage and fair distribution.
